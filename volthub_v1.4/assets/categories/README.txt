Put your category pictures here. The storefront shows them on the category tabs.
Name each file after the category: lowercase, spaces and symbols become dashes.
Accepted types: jpg, jpeg, png, webp, svg. A square image around 100x100 works well.

A category without a picture still works, it just shows the text only.
